Know before you play

2 amethyst crystals = a claim

☠️
Semi-hardcore

24-hour respawns. Your gravestone is locked for 48 hours.

⚔️
No combat logging

Log out mid-fight and a copy of you stays behind that can be killed.

⚠️
Ender OS computers are black boxes

They run a real web browser, and you can't see what another player has set up on theirs. Be cautious on other people's PCs: don't log into your real accounts, type passwords or wallet details, or trust links and downloads on them. Your screen can also be seen by nearby players who've turned PC screens on, and on projectors.
