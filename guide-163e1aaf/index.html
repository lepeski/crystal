Critters Field Guide

Where every critter lives on CrystalMath, what it eats, what it becomes and whether you can ride it. Read from the server's own spawn rules, so it's always current. Models, animations and spawn data come from Cobblemon.

Finding them

  • Wild critters are rare: roughly one find every 10–20 minutes of moving about, and they stay where they spawned.
  • Rarity is the weight once a spot fits: common, uncommon, rare, ultra rare.
  • No spawns near world spawn, inside claims or in torch-lit places. "Underground" means caves only; otherwise surface and caves both work.

Taming and catching

  • Feed a wild one its food. Each feed has the listed chance to win it over; evolved forms are harder.
  • Once it trusts you, your ball always catches it. An untamed one fights back.
  • Poké Ball: 3 red dye / iron, stone button, iron / 3 white dye.

Shinies

1 in 1024 wild critters is shiny, in its own colours (a shiny Charmander has a blue flame). You won't see a sparkle in the wild; it sparkles when it comes out of your ball. Its ball says "★ Shiny".

Legendaries

  • Only one of each about at a time, and very rare: about a day of searching in the right place.
  • The birds also fly across the sky now and then and land on the highest peak ahead. Follow them.
  • Tame with golden apples (slim odds) or enchanted golden apples (good odds).

Evolution stones and healing

  • Stones: 4 copper ingots around a core. Fire fire charge · Water prismarine crystals · Thunder lightning rod · Moon phantom membrane · Leaf moss block.
  • Healing machine: glass / iron, glistering melon, iron / iron, redstone block, iron. Needs an FM Radio computer beside it.

Riding

Right-click your own big critter with an empty hand. Fliers take off with a double-tap of jump from Lv. 36. Water mounts surf; land mounts paddle (slowly) in deep water. Sneak to get off.
